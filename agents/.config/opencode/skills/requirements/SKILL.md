## When to Load

First when user says ANY of:
- "build X", "create X", "make X"
- "add feature", "need X", "want X"
- "help me design", "help me plan"
- "new project", "from scratch"

## Workflow

1. Ask what - What do they want?
2. Ask why - Why do they need it?
3. Ask who - Who is this for?
4. Validate - Repeat back understanding
5. Create PBI - Use todowrite for requirements
